# Selected Papers

This file provides a curated list of key studies reviewed in this repository. The aim is to organise the literature on Concept-Based Explainable AI (C-XAI) for retinal and ophthalmic image analysis, with emphasis on methodological development, clinical relevance, reliability, and evaluation.

## 1. Foundational Concept-Based XAI Methods

These papers introduce or extend core concept-based explanation methods that support human-interpretable model reasoning.

- Testing with Concept Activation Vectors (TCAV)
- Automated Concept-based Explanations (ACE)
- Concept Bottleneck Models (CBMs)
- Concept Embedding Models (CEMs)
- Self-Explaining Neural Networks (SENN)
- Prototype-based models, including ProtoPNet
- ConceptSHAP and related concept-importance methods

## 2. Post-hoc Concept-Based Explanation Methods

These studies apply concept-based explanations after model training to interpret or audit black-box models.

- Class–concept relation methods
- Node–concept association methods
- Unsupervised concept discovery methods
- Causal concept explanation methods
- Concept completeness and concept importance evaluation

## 3. Explainable-by-Design Concept-Based Models

These works design models to reason through explicit or learned concepts during prediction.

- Supervised Concept Bottleneck Models
- Concept Embedding Models
- Prototype-based explainable models
- Logic-based and rule-based concept models
- Probabilistic and uncertainty-aware concept models

## 4. Retinal and Ophthalmic AI Studies

These papers focus on retinal imaging tasks where concept-level explanations can support clinical interpretation.

- Diabetic retinopathy detection and grading
- Fundus image classification
- Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT) analysis
- Lesion detection and localisation
- Optic disc, macula, vessel, and pathological concept analysis

## 5. Trustworthy and Clinically Reliable AI

These studies are relevant to the reliability, robustness, and clinical usability of concept-based explanations.

- Explanation faithfulness
- Explanation stability and consistency
- Robustness against artefacts and perturbations
- Human-in-the-loop evaluation
- Clinical alignment and medical knowledge grounding

## 6. Emerging Directions

This section will be updated with recent studies on next-generation retinal C-XAI.

- Vision-Language Models for retinal reasoning
- Knowledge Graph-guided explanations
- Generative concept auditing

- SMILE-based reliability auditing
