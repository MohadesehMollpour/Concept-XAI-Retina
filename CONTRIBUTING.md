# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in contributing to this repository.

This repository is intended to support research on Concept-Based Explainable AI (C-XAI) for retinal and ophthalmic image analysis. Contributions may include relevant papers, datasets, evaluation metrics, tools, or implementation resources.

## Suggested Contributions

You may suggest:

- New papers on Concept-Based XAI
- Retinal or ophthalmic AI datasets
- Evaluation metrics for explanation reliability
- Open-source tools related to medical XAI
- Corrections to taxonomy or method descriptions

## Contribution Guidelines

Please ensure that suggested resources are:

- Relevant to Concept-Based XAI, retinal imaging, ophthalmology, or trustworthy medical AI
- Scientifically credible
- Clearly cited
- Linked to the original paper, dataset, or official repository where possible

## Contact

For major suggestions or corrections, please open an issue or contact the repository maintainer.
